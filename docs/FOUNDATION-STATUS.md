## Operating boundaries

- Keep the organization limited to the five approved repositories.
- Use `main` and one commit per approved task.
- Do not add paid services, external infrastructure, integrations, or automation without explicit owner approval.
- Keep private core and roadmap information private.
- Treat future capabilities and maintenance work as separate tasks requiring their own approval.
