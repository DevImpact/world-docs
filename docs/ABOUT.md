# About DevImpact

## Overview

**DevImpact** is a GitHub-centered digital ecosystem created to organize software projects, publish useful resources, and provide a consistent public identity for development work.

The ecosystem uses a small set of repositories with clearly separated responsibilities. Public repositories present information and resources. Private repositories hold internal material that is not intended for public distribution.

## Mission

Our mission is to build and maintain useful digital products and developer resources through clear documentation, responsible engineering, and sustainable practices.

## What DevImpact is

- A shared identity for a collection of digital projects.
- A structured place to publish project information and documentation.
- A framework for maintaining consistent standards across repositories.
- A GitHub-based initiative designed to grow carefully over time.

## What DevImpact does not claim

The presence of an organization or brand on GitHub does not, by itself, establish a legally incorporated company, regulated service, or formal business relationship. Any such status must be established separately and accurately before it is represented publicly.

## Official channels

- GitHub organization: https://github.com/DevImpact
- Main website: https://devimpact.github.io/
- Public project directory: https://github.com/DevImpact/world-directory
- Documentation source: https://github.com/DevImpact/world-docs

## Scope

The initial ecosystem is intentionally limited to five repositories. New repositories, external platforms, paid services, integrations, and automated processes are outside the approved scope unless the owner explicitly authorizes them.
